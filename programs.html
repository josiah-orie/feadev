Programs

Comprehensive programs for athletes of all skill levels—from grassroots beginners to elite performers preparing for international opportunities.

Each AFEDEV program is designed with clear outcomes, qualified coaching, and pathways that connect performance on the field with education and career readiness.

Wheelchair American Football Academy

Our specialized adaptive sports program is designed specifically for para-athletes. It provides structured training in Wheelchair American Football, focusing on building athleticism, team coordination, and strategic play in an inclusive and supportive environment.

  • Wheelchair propulsion, maneuverability, and chair safety
  • Adaptive throwing, receiving, and blocking progressions
  • Offensive and defensive chair positioning and team schemes
  • Inclusive strength, conditioning, and athletic development
  • Leadership, team communication, and strategic film sessions

Program eligibility: Open to para-athletes of all age categories (placement based on assessment)

Youth Academy Program

Foundational training for ages 10–18 focused on skills, teamwork, discipline, and athletic development. Ideal for athletes new to American football or building core competencies before advanced pathways.

  • Position fundamentals and safe technique progression
  • Strength and conditioning appropriate to age group
  • Character and leadership training
  • Academic support and study skills coaching

Age categories: U12 • U15 • U18 (placement by assessment)

School Outreach Program

AFEDEV partners with primary and secondary schools to introduce American football through clinics, workshops, and after-school programs—building participation safely and sustainably.

  • Introductory clinics for students and PE staff
  • After-school training blocks
  • Equipment and safety guidance for institutions
  • Pathways into academy and combine programs

Football Combine & Showcases

Annual event where athletes demonstrate performance metrics before local and international scouts. Includes timed drills, position work, and interview readiness.

  • 40-yard dash, agility, and power testing
  • Position-specific evaluations
  • Highlight film and profile support
  • Scout and recruiter attendance (as scheduled)
