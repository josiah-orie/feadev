# Product Overview

AFEDEV (American Football Elite Development Ltd) is a static marketing website for a Nigerian sports development organization dedicated to growing American football in Nigeria.

## Purpose

Promote AFEDEV's services and programs to prospective athletes, parents, partners, sponsors, and community members. The site communicates the organization's five pillars:

1. Sports Development — elite training, camps, athletic performance
2. Education & Mentorship — academic support, leadership programs
3. Advisory & Career Services — scholarships, recruitment guidance
4. Media & Content Production — documentaries, tutorials, storytelling
5. Events & Competitions — tournaments, showcases, scouting

## Target Audience

- Youth athletes (ages 10–18) and their parents/guardians
- Schools and educational institutions
- Corporate sponsors and partners
- Community organizations and NGOs
- Coaches and sports educators

## Key Calls to Action

- Join the Academy (athlete enrollment)
- Partner With Us (sponsorship/collaboration)
- Contact (general enquiries via mailto link)
- Newsletter subscription (placeholder, not yet connected)
