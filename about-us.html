About AFEDEV

Transforming American football in Nigeria through structured development pathways, youth engagement, and high-performance training that meets global standards.

Introduction

AFEDEV is committed to building a sustainable American football ecosystem in Africa—combining elite sports development with education, mentorship, advisory services, and community programs that nurture complete athletes and responsible leaders.

As a Nigerian-registered private company, we operate with professional governance, transparent partnerships, and a long-term vision for athlete excellence and national socio-economic contribution.

Our Story

From identifying a gap in structured football development to building a pioneering academy model—AFEDEV was founded to create lasting pathways for young athletes.

The need
American football was growing in interest across Nigeria, but lacked consistent coaching standards, academic integration, and scouting infrastructure.
Inspiration
Founders committed to a long-term model combining sports excellence, education, and community uplift—mirroring international development frameworks adapted for Africa.
Establishment
AFEDEV was incorporated as American Football Elite Development Ltd, registered in Nigeria, with a mandate spanning training, education, advisory, media, and events.
Milestones
Pilot programs launched, school partnerships formed, and the first academy-style training cycles introduced—laying groundwork for combines, coach education, and scholarship pathways.
