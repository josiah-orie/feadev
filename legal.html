Privacy Policy

American Football Elite Development Ltd (“AFEDEV”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through our website forms, academy registration, events, and partner inquiries.

  • Information we may collect: name, email, phone, organization, athlete details, and messages you submit.
  • How we use it: to respond to inquiries, manage programs, improve services, and communicate updates you opt into.
  • Data sharing: we do not sell personal data; sharing occurs only with service providers or partners necessary to deliver programs, under appropriate safeguards.
  • Retention: data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
  • Your rights: you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting football@feadev.com.

Safeguarding Policy (Youth Programs)

AFEDEV is committed to safeguarding children and vulnerable participants in all academy, school, and community programs.

  • All coaches and staff undergo appropriate background checks where required by law.
  • Clear reporting channels exist for concerns about welfare or misconduct.
  • Training environments follow age-appropriate supervision ratios and safety protocols.
  • Photography and media use require consent from guardians for minors.

Report safeguarding concerns to football@feadev.com with subject line “Safeguarding”.
